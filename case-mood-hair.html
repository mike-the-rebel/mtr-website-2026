01. The Context

A point of difference
they already had

A new owner had taken over an existing studio run by a team of three, and needed a rebrand and positioning to go with the change. At the time it was positioned like any other salon in the area, with nothing distinguishing it from its competitors.

Discovery covered business goals, target clientele and how the brand needed to be perceived. The insight came out of it: most competitors build their business around colouring and general styling, treating haircuts as an occasional add-on. Mood specialised in haircut styling, a genuine point of difference, not a claimed one.

03. Reflection

Looking
back

What worked

The discovery session. The positioning came straight out of what the team already did better than anyone nearby, so nothing had to be invented for it.

What I'd do differently

Three stylescapes was one too many. Two clear directions would have got us to the mark faster; the 3rd option mostly muddied the conversation.
