01. The Context

A name worth
standing for

In 2020 my mum came to me with an idea for a candle company. Her original name, Sierra Meadows Candle Co, was one of many. I proposed "Adulting" instead because it came from her own story, becoming a parent earlier than planned and building a life without a roadmap. It wasn't a joke; it was literal, and it gave the brand something to actually stand for.

I designed the identity and built the first site in my own time, then rebuilt the whole thing on Shopify six years in: full navigation, sections, HTML templates and web graphics, all across two weekends.

Built overseas, on a family budget, with no photographer and no team to delegate to.

03. Outcome

From 136 scents
to a shortlist

The rebuild made products easier to find and filter across the growing catalogue.

It also made choosing a candle scent easier. Customers can filter the 136-scent library by family down to 5–10 options to choose from, instead of scrolling the full list.

04. Reflection

Looking
back

What worked

Pushing back on the first name to land on a more meaningful and unique name that has led the brand to be more memorable. The site redesign helped organise the growing product catalogue and enormous 136 fragrance library into an easy to navigate and find your ideal scent.

What I'd do differently

I would have blocked out dedicated time for this earlier, rather than fitting it around other commitments. With more time up front, I likely would have migrated the site to Shopify sooner too, once I saw the growth the fragrance and product catalogue would have.
