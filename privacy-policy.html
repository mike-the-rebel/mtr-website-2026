Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026. This outlines how enquiries and project information shared with Mike the Rebel are handled.

What you send me

When you get in touch by email, I collect the details you send me directly: your name, contact info, and anything about your project.

Site analytics

This site uses Google Tag Manager to run analytics, which uses cookies to see how the site is used. It records things like which pages are viewed, which case studies are opened, and when a contact or outbound link is clicked. This data is anonymous and is only used to understand what's working on the site. I don't use advertising pixels or retargeting.

You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics with Google's opt-out tool.

Your theme choice

If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your browser so the site remembers it next time. It isn't sent anywhere.

How it's used

Information you share is used only to respond to your enquiry and, if we work together, to deliver the project. I don't sell or share your details with third parties, other than the analytics services described above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@miketherebel.com.
