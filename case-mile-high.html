01. The Context

What I build when
nobody's asking

A subscription coffee brand built around altitude, exploration and aviation. I'd been sitting on the idea for about three years. A final-year assignment gave me the excuse to actually build it, so I put the system together over a few weekends around work and study.

The altitude angle isn't only aesthetic. Coffee grown at high elevation tends to develop more complex flavour notes, so there's something real to talk about. It also happens to be the elevation of my hometown, Reno, which is where the idea for the name came from.

02. What I Built

What I
Built

A full brand system, self-directed.

01
Identity
A hand-drawn wordmark set in a golden ring, styled after the stain a coffee cup leaves behind. Sigmar with Montserrat; a palette drawn from coffee itself.
02
A Functional Prototype
Built in HTML, CSS and JS rather than stopped at static comps: three subscription tiers, switching between beans and capsules, and a Brew Match quiz that turns a few answers into a real product recommendation.
03
Packaging System
Bag, box and capsule, with accent colours distinguishing each origin and roast across the range.
03. Reflection

Looking
back

What worked

The visual identity system, colour coordination across beans, and consistency carried through packaging, pods, billboards, the site and promo items. It reads as one brand across every touchpoint, not a set of matching assets.

What I'd do differently

I'd have liked more time to develop the brand further. Between work, study, and migrating to Australia, time has been scarce. I built what I could around everything else rather than giving it the runway it deserved.
