01. The Challenge

A trusted name,
an outdated pitch.

ADRAD has built a reputation across the Australian automotive trade for reliable radiators and cooling systems. But the product catalogue and trade communications hadn't kept pace: a dense, dated print document doing the job of a modern digital campaign.

Distributors respected the brand. They just weren't hearing from it in a way that drove action. The business wanted a sharper, more contemporary way to bring a major trade promotion to market.

One Campaign.
One Focused Day.

Trade Promotion Campaign
02. The Strategy

One day.
Total focus.

Rather than a slow-drip campaign, we built the entire push around a single trade promotion day, concentrating attention, urgency, and incentive into one unmissable moment for ADRAD's distributor network.

The redesigned catalogue became the campaign's centrepiece: clearer product hierarchy, sharper photography direction, and a layout built to convert browsing into ordering, both in print and as a digital flip-through.

"The catalogue redesign and campaign gave our distributor network exactly the kind of moment we were looking for."
Trade Marketing Lead, ADRAD
03. The Process

Built to
convert.

The campaign ran across coordinated channels: a redesigned catalogue as the core reference document, a multi-stage EDM sequence building anticipation ahead of the promotion day, and dedicated landing pages for order capture.

Every touchpoint pointed distributors toward the same moment. No competing messages, no diluted urgency: one clear campaign, built to peak on one day.

04. The Outcome

A record day
for trade orders.

The promotion day delivered the strongest single-day trade order result in the campaign's history, a result that reframed how the team thought about campaign-led marketing versus steady-state promotion.

The redesigned catalogue is now the standing reference document across the distributor network, and the single-day campaign format has become a repeatable playbook for future promotions.
