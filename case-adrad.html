01. The Context

Building the system,
not just the artwork

Adrad Holdings (ASX: AHL) spans Adrad, Natrad, Natrad Trade, Natrad HDC and Air Radiators. I'm one of three designers across the Distribution division, and the work here runs well beyond the scope of the role: brand systems, campaign strategy, and internal tools built for the marketing team.

No formal brand guidelines, photographer or request pipeline, and weekly promotional deadlines. So I built the missing infrastructure first, then designed on top of it, as well as an analytical tracking system to inform future creative decisions.

04. Reflection

Looking
back

What worked

Building the templated promo system enabled us to launch multiple catalogues in any given month. What used to take 3+ weeks for a single catalogue now takes less than 1 week. The bonus is that other members from the team can run these systems without me in the room.

What I'd do differently

I would have set up the GA4 and Flipsnack catalogue tracking early to capture data on the performance of our first 3 One Day Sale campaigns. This could help us identify weaknesses in our marketing efforts and help us improve our approach based on behavioral data, in conjunction with sales data.
